Two renders of the same data — compare index-spaced vs. value-scaled x-axis.

A. Index-spaced x-axis (current sweep-chart.html)

Balloon counts placed at equal intervals regardless of value — 50→100 and 800→1600 get the same visual width.

B. Linear value-scaled x-axis

Balloon counts placed proportional to their numeric value — 1600 balloons sits 4x further right than 400.

Because the swept balloon counts double each step (50, 100, 200, 400, 800, 1600), a linear scale crushes the first four points into the left ~25% of the chart and stretches 800→1600 across the rest — it visually buries exactly the transition zone (200→800) that the index-spaced version makes legible.
