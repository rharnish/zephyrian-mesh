Zephyrian Dirigibles · mesh comms

No balloon is ever told that it is connected.

Balloons drift on real ERA5 winds. Each one carries a radio, and has to work out from scratch whether it can still reach the ground — using nothing but the signals that physically arrived at its antenna.

beacon
A tower's periodic shout: who I am, which wave this is, zero hops so far.
hop count
How many relays a beacon crossed to reach you. Lower is a better route.
next hop
The single neighbour you heard the best beacon from. Where your telemetry goes.
belief expiry
Beliefs age out on how old the news is, not on when it was last repeated.
union-find
The simulator's ground truth. Balloons have no access to it, ever.

Before any signal arrives, being deep inside a healthy mesh and being alone over the Pacific are indistinguishable to a balloon. That gap — between simulating a network and simulating knowledge of a network — is the whole exercise.
